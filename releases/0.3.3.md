# Pocket Agent v0.3.3

**Release Date**: TBD

## Highlights
- Voiceprints now have their own page in Settings, with a sample for the computer microphone and an optional one for your mobile device's microphone. Wake, interruption and "Only listen to my voice" in conversations use the sample for where PA is listening: on the computer, the computer sample; on a mobile device, its own sample if you recorded one, otherwise the computer sample. A mobile device sample stops the mobile device from rejecting you, and you can delete it to go back to the computer sample. The mobile device sample is recorded from the computer's Settings while the device is connected with the PA page open. The page also holds the voice match strictness (moved from Wake word) and a "Test my voice" button that shows the score for each sample and whether you pass on the sample in use. The sample name dialog is gone.

## Fixes
- Phone: swiping back from the tools and reasoning page now works anywhere on the page, including on the tool and reasoning boxes (before, only the gaps between them responded).
- Wake phrases ending in a made-up word with a final "y", such as "hey pocky", never woke Pocket Agent: the detector listened for "pock-why". It now listens for "pock-ee". The Wake word settings also show, under the wake phrase, what the detector actually listens for.
- Voiceprint strictness for the wake word now defaults to 35% (was 50%): a mobile device's microphone in call mode changes your voice enough that 50% often rejected the owner. If you have saved settings before, your current value is kept; change it under Settings → Voiceprint → Voice match strictness.
- The main window and the phone page now follow the interface language in Settings (all 7 languages). Before, thinking and tool steps, error messages, the computer-action approval box, the right-click menu, and the phone page's buttons and connection notices always showed Chinese. Tool steps now read "Using {tool}" (on screen and when spoken) instead of "Calling {tool}".

## Upgrading
- If your voice is often rejected on a mobile device, open Settings → Voiceprint and record a mobile device sample while the device is connected and showing the PA page. Your existing voiceprint keeps working as the computer sample.
