# Pocket Agent v0.3.3

**Release Date**: TBD

## Fixes
- Phone: swiping back from the tools and reasoning page now works anywhere on the page, including on the tool and reasoning boxes (before, only the gaps between them responded).
- Wake phrases ending in a made-up word with a final "y", such as "hey pocky", never woke Pocket Agent: the detector listened for "pock-why". It now listens for "pock-ee". The Wake word settings also show, under the wake phrase, what the detector actually listens for.
- Voiceprint strictness for the wake word now defaults to 35% (was 50%): the phone microphone in call mode changes your voice enough that 50% often rejected the owner. If you have saved settings before, your current value is kept; change it under Settings → Wake word → Voice match strictness.
